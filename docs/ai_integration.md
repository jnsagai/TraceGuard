# AI Integration

TraceGuard consumes structured AI proposal files. It does not call AI models and does not accept AI confidence as a final coverage decision.

AI may propose requirements, parent links, covered atoms, derivation types, and rationales. TraceGuard validates those artifacts deterministically.
